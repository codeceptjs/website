Writing about CodeceptJS, linking to it, or building an integration? Everything
on this page is free to use — and if you're publishing an article or post, a
link back to [codecept.io](/) is always appreciated.

We'd just ask one thing: keep the logo recognizable, so readers can still tell
it's CodeceptJS.
